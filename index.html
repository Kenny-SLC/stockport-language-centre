Our ESOL Pilot Programme

Now Recruiting: 6-week ESOL cohorts (Pre-A1 and A1, separate) starting 1st Monday in July 2026

📚

What You'll Learn

English for UC & Work: Claiming benefits, attending appointments, job interviews, workplace communication, digital skills—all in real context.

💻

How It Works

Online via Teams: 2 × 90-min sessions/week + homework. Two levels: Pre-A1 (beginner) and A1 (early intermediate). Aligned to CEFR framework.

💼

Work Experience Included

Week 6 → Work Placement: Finish your course with real workplace experience through our partner organisations.

Why Stockport Language Centre?
