ESOL Pilot Programme

Free 6-Week Intensive English Course

For Universal Credit claimants in Stockport
Starts: 1st Monday in July 2026

Learn How to Apply

What is the ESOL Pilot?

A free, 6-week intensive English course designed specifically for Universal Credit claimants. We teach English in the context of your UC journey, job search, and workplace communication—not classroom grammar.

You study online via Teams with a small group (max 12 learners), so you get plenty of speaking practice and personal support.

Key benefit: Week 6 leads into a work-experience placement, so you finish the course with real workplace language practice and something concrete for your CV.

Who is it for?

✅

UC Claimants

You're claiming Universal Credit in Stockport or Greater Manchester

✅

English Language Learners

Your first language is not English, and you're at Pre-A1 or A1 level

✅

Job-Ready

You want to work on your English to improve job prospects and workplace confidence

You don't need formal education experience. We meet you where you are and build your confidence from day one.

What You'll Learn

🎯 English for UC & Appointments

  • Claiming benefits and understanding your rights
  • Attending Job Centre appointments with confidence
  • Filling forms (UC, job applications, etc.)
  • Phone calls and email communication

💼 English for Work & Interviews

  • Job interview language and confidence
  • CV writing and application letters
  • Workplace communication and instructions
  • Teamwork, questions, and asking for help

💻 Digital Skills Built In

Alongside English, you'll build confidence using Teams, email, job websites, online forms, and other digital tools you need in modern workplaces.

How It Works

📅 Schedule

  • Dates: Starts 1st Monday in July 2026 (6 weeks)
  • Classes: 2 sessions per week, 90 minutes each (online via Teams)
  • Homework: ~45 minutes per week (optional but recommended)
  • Two levels: Pre-A1 (beginner) and A1 (early intermediate)
  • Max group size: 12 learners (so you get personal attention)

🎓 Week-by-Week Breakdown

Weeks 1–3: Foundation

Greetings, personal information, basic UC vocabulary, numbers, dates, essential phrases. Building confidence and speaking time.

Weeks 4–5: UC & Job Readiness

Filling forms, phone calls to Job Centre, interview language, job applications, workplace communication. Real-world practice.

Week 6: Consolidation & Placement Planning

CEFR assessment (see your progress!), learner feedback, transition planning for work-experience placement (starts Week 7).

💼 Work-Experience Placement (Weeks 6+)

This is what makes our programme different. In Week 6, we work with our partners (like Stockport Homes) to find you a volunteer or work-experience placement suited to your level and interests.

What this might look like: Admin support, customer service, resident support, community work, or any role where you'll practise real workplace English. You're speaking, learning on the job, and building your CV.
  • Placement is voluntary work (unpaid, but great CV experience)
  • We prepare you in Week 6 so you feel confident
  • Kenneth stays involved to support you
  • Typically 2–3 days per week for a few weeks

How We Measure Progress

When You Start

  • Take our online level assessment (10 minutes, no pass/fail)
  • We assess your English using the CEFR framework (Pre-A1 → A1 → etc.)

When You Finish (Week 6)

  • CEFR reassessment – see your progress!
  • Learner feedback – we want to know what worked and what didn't
  • Certificate of completion – something concrete for your CV
  • Transition to placement – we support you moving into work experience

Frequently Asked Questions

Is it really free?

Yes. During the pilot phase (first two cohorts), the programme is completely free. We're gathering evidence to apply for ongoing funding.

What if I'm not confident using Teams?

That's OK! We'll do a practice session before Week 1 to make sure you're comfortable. Teams is straightforward, and we'll teach you.

What if I have other commitments during the 6 weeks?

Let us know. The cohorts are designed to be flexible. If you miss a session, we'll share the recording and homework separately. But attendance is important for group learning and speaking practice.

What if my English is lower than Pre-A1?

Our Pre-A1 cohort is designed for absolute beginners. But get in touch with Kenneth—we can discuss whether the timing is right or if you'd benefit from some preparation.

What happens after the 6 weeks if I want to continue?

Great! We're planning to run ongoing cohorts and apply for funding post-pilot. We'll keep you updated on next steps and further learning options.

Will I get a certificate?

Yes. You'll receive a certificate of completion plus your CEFR assessment result—something concrete for your CV and job applications.
