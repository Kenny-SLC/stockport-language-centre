Partnership with Stockport Homes

ESOL & Employment Support for Your UC Tenant Residents

Why This Matters for Stockport Homes

Your UC claimant residents face a specific barrier: language skills for employment. They need English for UC appointments, job interviews, and workplace communication. But generic ESOL courses feel disconnected from their actual journey.

We've built something different—aligned specifically to the UC pathway and employment outcomes. And we're looking to partner with Stockport Homes because you understand your residents' needs better than anyone.

The Reality for Your Residents

Your UC claimant residents with ESOL needs struggle with:

  • Attending Job Centre appointments with confidence
  • Filling job application forms and CVs in English
  • Understanding UC letters, documents, and procedures
  • Job interviews and workplace communication
  • Building employment confidence and resilience

The gap: They know they need English for work, but generic ESOL courses don't connect to their immediate UC journey. They drop out because it doesn't feel relevant.

How the Partnership Works

Step 1: Referral (Pilot Phase)

Your frontline staff (Resident Support Team, Housing Advisors, etc.) identify UC claimants with ESOL needs. They're motivated to work, but language is a barrier. You refer them to us with basic info (name, contact, context).

Step 2: Assessment & Placement

They take our online level assessment (10 minutes). We confirm Pre-A1 or A1, send joining details, they start in the next cohort. We send you confirmation they're enrolled.

Step 3: Learning (6 weeks)

2 sessions per week, online Teams. We send you monthly updates: attendance, progress, any concerns. At Week 6, they take CEFR reassessment. We share the result with you.

Step 4: Feedback (Pilot)

After Week 6, we ask you: "How did your referral get on? Any observations on readiness for work?" We gather this feedback for pilot evaluation and GMCA applications.

Step 5: Full Course & Placements (September+)

If your resident wants to continue, they can. 9-week course. Week 10 is voluntary placement. You and us work together to identify suitable roles. They get work experience. You assess their employment readiness.

Week 10 Placements (Full Course, September+)

Why placements matter: After 9 weeks of learning, your residents need real-world practice. Week 10 placement gives them:

  • Real workplace English: Speaking to colleagues, taking instructions, problem-solving
  • Employment confidence: They see they CAN work in an English-speaking environment
  • CV experience: "Volunteer/Work Experience at [Organisation]" – concrete evidence for future employers
  • Assessment by you: You see how ready they are for actual employment

What placements could look like:

  • Admin or office support (filing, data entry, phones)
  • Resident support roles (helping other residents)
  • Community-facing work (events, welcome desk)
  • Team-based roles (warehousing, maintenance, with team communication)

Time commitment: 2–3 days per week, 4–6 weeks. Flexible. Afternoons or full days depending on your needs.

Support: We prepare them in Week 9. Weekly check-ins with learner and you. Problem-solving if needed. Feedback form at the end.
