About Stockport Language Centre

Stockport Language Centre CIC is a community interest company dedicated to making ESOL education accessible, practical, and aligned to the real needs of Universal Credit claimants in Greater Manchester.

Meet Kenneth Moore, Founder & Lead Teacher

Kenneth brings 30+ years of IT experience and a deep understanding of how compliance, training, and language work together to help people succeed.

For 13 years at Fujitsu, Kenneth worked in Data Protection and professional awareness training, designing courses that made complex topics accessible to real people. He knows how to explain things clearly—and how to make learning stick.

As a UC Work Coach at DWP, Kenneth sees firsthand what jobseekers need: not just English lessons, but English *in context* of UC, job interviews, and workplace communication. He understands the UC journey from the inside.

He founded Stockport Language Centre CIC to bridge a gap: quality ESOL teaching aligned to actual employment outcomes—delivered with respect for learners' time and real-world constraints.

Why We Started This

Kenneth saw a pattern in his work at the Job Centre:

  • UC claimants with ESOL needs often have no straightforward path to local English courses aligned to their job search
  • Existing ESOL provision is often generic, classroom-based, and disconnected from the real language UC claimants need (appointments, forms, interviews, workplace communication)
  • Work coaches and support workers have no easy way to refer claimants to quality ESOL that fits into their UC journey
  • Learners drop out when they don't see direct relevance to employment
"I wanted to create something different: ESOL that's practical, relevant, and designed around how people actually learn best—high speaking time, real tasks, and a pathway to work."

Our Approach

🎯

Real-World English

We teach English in the context of UC, job interviews, workplace communication—not abstract grammar.

👥

Everybody Speaks

High speaking time. Small groups. Flexible pacing. We make sure every learner gets practice and confidence.

💼

Employment-Focused

We align everything to the UC journey and job readiness. Week 6 includes work-experience placement.

🤝

Partnership-Built

We work with frontline organisations—Jobcentre Plus, Citizens Advice, Stockport Homes, refugee support—to make referrals and placements seamless.

📊

Evidence-Led

We measure progress using CEFR, learner feedback, and employment outcomes. We're transparent about what works.

🌱

Inclusive & Accessible

Free during pilot. Online delivery. Flexible cohort sizes. We remove barriers to access.

Community Interest Company

Stockport Language Centre is a Community Interest Company (CIC). This means:

  • Not for profit: Any surplus is reinvested into the programme or used to keep costs low for learners
  • Community-focused: Our mission is to support ESOL learners in Stockport and Greater Manchester, not to maximize profit
  • Transparent & accountable: We report on our activities, outcomes, and finances to the Community Interest Company Regulator
  • Sustainable: We're building partnerships and seeking funding (GMCA grants, charities, Jobcentre Flexible Support Fund) to scale sustainably
