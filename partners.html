Partner With Us

Are you a Jobcentre, Citizens Advice, Stockport Homes, or community organisation?

We're building partnerships to refer UC claimants with ESOL needs to our free pilot programme and provide work-experience placements.

Let's close a gap together.

The Problem We're Solving

You work with UC claimants every day. You see them struggling with:

  • English language barriers to job search and interviews
  • Difficulty completing job applications, CVs, and forms
  • No local, work-focused ESOL courses that fit their UC journey
  • Lack of work experience to build employment confidence

You refer them to generic ESOL courses, but they don't always stick—because the connection to their job search feels distant.

We've built something different: 6-week ESOL aligned to UC, job-readiness, and real workplace placement. Free. Practical. Results-focused.

What Partnership Looks Like

👥

Learner Referrals

You identify UC claimants with ESOL needs and refer them to our programme. We handle assessment, teaching, and progress tracking.

💼

Work-Experience Placements

You provide volunteer or work-experience placements for learners in Week 6+. They practise real workplace English while you assess their employment readiness.

📊

Feedback & Outcomes

You get regular updates on your referrals' progress, CEFR improvements, and placement outcomes—evidence of impact.

How the Partnership Works

1. You Refer a Learner

A UC claimant in your service has ESOL needs and wants to improve their English for work. You complete a simple referral form (name, contact, brief context) and send it to Kenneth.

2. We Assess & Place

They take our online level assessment. We confirm they're Pre-A1 or A1, send them joining details for Teams, and they start in the next cohort.

3. They Learn & Build Confidence

6 weeks of online classes (2 × 90 min/week). We email you with attendance and progress updates. At Week 6, they take a CEFR reassessment (we share the result).

4. You Provide a Placement

In Week 6, we discuss placement options with you. You offer a volunteer or work-experience role (2–3 days/week). Kenneth supports them and you assess their readiness.

5. Placement & Beyond

They move into your placement (Weeks 7+). You see their workplace English in action. Kenneth stays available for troubleshooting. You provide feedback at the end.

About Work-Experience Placements

What kinds of placements work?

Any role where learners will speak English and build workplace confidence:

  • Admin or office support – filing, data entry, phone calls
  • Customer-facing roles – resident support, reception, community work
  • Team-based roles – warehouse, maintenance support, cleaning (with team communication)
  • Volunteer roles – community events, support groups, mentoring

How much time commitment?

2–3 days per week for 4–6 weeks. Flexible, based on learner and organisation needs. Can be afternoons or full days.

What support do we provide?

  • Pre-placement prep: We prepare learners in Week 6 (what to expect, key phrases, questions to ask)
  • Weekly check-ins: Kenneth calls or emails the learner and you each week to see how it's going
  • Problem-solving: If something isn't working, we troubleshoot together
  • Feedback: You complete a simple feedback form at the end (how ready for employment are they?)

Our Target Partners

We're prioritising partnerships with organisations that have direct contact with UC claimants:

  • Jobcentre Plus Stockport – referrals from work coaches
  • Citizens Advice Stockport – claimants seeking support
  • Stockport Homes – resident support, placements
  • Refugee & asylum support – Stockport Signpost, Refugee Action GM
  • Children's Centres & Family Hubs – parents returning to work
  • Mosques & community centres – community reach

But we're open to partnerships with any organisation aligned to supporting UC claimants or ESOL learners.

Getting Started

Next step: Get in touch with Kenneth. We'll discuss your needs, how referrals work, and what placements might look like. No commitment needed—just a conversation.

What You'll Need from Us

  • One-pager (to share with your frontline staff) – explains what we do, how to refer
  • Referral form (simple: name, contact, UC status, ESOL needs)
  • Ongoing communication – we keep you updated on referrals and placements

What We Need from You

  • Referrals – UC claimants aged 18+ with ESOL needs and motivation to work
  • Placements (optional, but valuable) – 2–3 days/week for 4–6 weeks, supervised by your team
  • Feedback – brief update on placement and learner readiness at the end
