About This Partnership

We're proposing a strategic pilot partnership with Stockport Homes to deliver targeted ESOL provision for Stockport Homes tenants. Our programme focuses on practical English for navigating housing, Universal Credit, job hunting, NHS services, and digital literacy.

This is a genuine pilot—we're co-designing with Stockport Homes, gathering rigorous outcome data, and building a model that works for your tenants.

Quick Facts

  • 10-week rolling cohorts (10–12 learners)
  • Entry Level 1 (A1) → A1 Secure progression
  • £499 per learner
  • Online delivery (2 x 90-min sessions/week)
  • Week 10 volunteering placements

What We Deliver

📚

Bespoke Curriculum

Co-designed with Stockport Homes, focused on housing, UC, jobs, NHS, and digital skills that tenants actually need.

👥

Experienced Tutor

Qualified ESOL educator with culturally responsive teaching and high student talk time (60%+ of lesson).

💻

Online & Accessible

Fully online via Microsoft Teams. No transport barriers = high attendance. Flexible for cultural/religious needs.

📊

Rigorous Outcomes

Track attendance, language progression, UC/housing/employment skills, digital literacy, placement success.

🤝

Week 10 Volunteering

Learners volunteer with Stockport Homes, gaining work experience. You get volunteer labour + assessment data.

📈

Partner with Evidence

Funder-ready outcome reports strengthen your GMCA, Big Lottery, and trust fund applications.

Curriculum Topics

🏠 Housing & Tenancy

Tenancy agreements, repair requests, landlord communication, tenant rights, neighbourhood relations.

💷 Universal Credit

Navigating UC portal, reporting changes, understanding entitlements, recording commitments, appeals.

💼 Job Hunting

CVs, job applications, interview skills, workplace communication, employment rights.

🏥 NHS & Health

Booking appointments, prescriptions, health information, mental health services access.

💻 Digital Skills

Microsoft 365, Teams, email, online job portals, online banking basics.

🎯 Confidence & Independence

Assertive communication, asking for help, navigating systems, building social connections.

What We Measure

Rigorous outcome tracking across multiple dimensions:

Language Progression

Pre-A1 entry → A1 Secure exit. Learners can reliably and independently handle practical English.

UC Skills

Navigate portal, understand entitlements, record commitments, manage UC journal independently.

Housing Skills

Read & understand tenancy agreements, submit repair requests, communicate with landlord.

Employment Readiness

CV completion, job application forms, interview confidence, workplace communication.

Digital Literacy

Teams video conferencing, email, online forms, online banking, job portals.

Placement Success

Hours completed, supervisor feedback, learner confidence, employment pathways.

Partnership Terms

What Stockport Homes Provides

  • Funding: £499 per learner per cohort
  • Learner referral: 10–12 per cohort
  • Co-design input on curriculum
  • Host Week 10 volunteering placements
  • Comms support (newsletter mentions, tenant comms)
  • Permission to use outcomes in grant applications

What We Provide

  • Bespoke curriculum & all materials
  • Experienced, culturally responsive tutor
  • Weekly progress updates
  • Mid-cohort feedback calls (Week 5)
  • Placement coordination & check-ins
  • Funder-ready outcomes reports
  • Co-branded materials & visibility
